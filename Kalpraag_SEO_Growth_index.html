The strategic thesis

/02 — NARRATIVE
Primary Growth Lever

Organic search is the highest-leverage acquisition channel for the next four quarters.

Kalpraag has earned genuine category authority in designer men's ethnic wear — 5.9M annual SERP impressions, 91K clicks, page-1 visibility on category-defining terms like "designer kurta for men" (pos. 5.4) and "open jodhpuri" (pos. 4.0). The brand's organic users convert at ₹90 per new user vs ₹14 for paid social. The volume gap between current organic performance and addressable demand is the entire opportunity in this report.

  • Non-brand impressions are 93% of total — the demand is there, the rankings are not
  • Top non-brand keywords sit at positions 5–10 — close enough that small lifts compound
  • Each ranking page is a permanent acquisition asset at zero marginal CAC
Three Levers, In Order
  • Collection-page SEO — Kurta & Jodhpuri collections already drive 13K+ & 7.9K clicks. They sit at positions 9–13. A position lift to top-5 doubles the click volume.
  • Technical foundation — 169 pages with invalid head HTML, 100 canonicalised pages, 4 broken internal links. Fix once, unlock site-wide.
  • Editorial moat — One blog post drives 9,981 clicks. Build the cluster around it — wedding wear, jodhpuri styling, celebrity-inspired looks.

The /jodhpuri-1 URL anomaly

The flagship Jodhpuri collection lives at /collections/jodhpuri-1 rather than the cleaner /collections/jodhpuri. The "-1" suffix typically indicates a Shopify duplicate-handle conflict from a prior collection migration. Both URLs exist; /jodhpuri-1 drives 7,903 clicks while /jodhpuri only drives 480 clicks. The split is splitting authority.

Fix path → Identify whichever has the stronger backlink profile (likely /jodhpuri-1), 301 redirect the other to it, consolidate internal navigation links, and update the Shopify handle to remove the "-1" if possible without breaking the redirect chain. Single highest-leverage on-page fix in this section.
⊕

Collection-page playbook — the four levers that move position

1. Category description copy (300–500 words above products). Currently most collections are bare product grids with no introductory copy. Add a category narrative — what defines a Kalpraag jodhpuri, who it's for, the craft story — before the product feed. This alone targets the long-tail variants the page already shows for.

2. ItemList + BreadcrumbList schema. Currently the collection pages emit basic product schema only. ItemList markup makes the collection eligible for rich grid SERP features.

3. Internal linking from blogs to collections. The "26 top ethnic wear brands" post drives 9,981 clicks — most of those readers should land in /collections/kurta or /collections/celebrity-closet. Currently blog→collection internal linking is sparse.

4. FAQ block per collection. "Difference between jodhpuri and sherwani", "How to style mirror work kurta", "Best kurta for haldi" — each becomes FAQ schema and captures featured-snippet eligibility.
